\# Curriculum Audit and Redesign — Task 2



\## Overview



This repository contains my curriculum audit for the B.Tech CSE (Artificial Intelligence) programme. It compares my curriculum and learning experience with selected entry-level job descriptions and recommends improvements to practical learning.



\## Contents



\- `report/` — Complete Task 2 report in Word and Markdown formats.

\- `evidence/` — Curriculum inventory, job-description comparison, curriculum recommendations, and AI/ML exercise log.

\- `interactive/` — Interactive curriculum comparison and credit-change calculator.

\- `project/` — Documentation for my independently developed GenAI Weather Intelligence System.



\## Methodology



The audit combines my learning experience, selected job descriptions, curriculum analysis, and AI-assisted exercises. The report documents the sources, verification steps, limitations, and proposed curriculum improvements.



\## Independent project



My GenAI Weather Intelligence System uses the OpenWeather and Groq APIs. Its role in this audit is to demonstrate independent project work and identify opportunities for more practical AI application development in the curriculum.



\## Limitations



The audit uses a sample of three job descriptions. Faculty and graduate feedback was not obtained. The proposed credit reallocation must be mapped to the official programme credit ledger before formal implementation.
